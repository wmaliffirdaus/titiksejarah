## Setting the cover
- **TikTok app:** after selecting the video, tap **Edit cover**. If the app offers to add a cover from your gallery or photos, choose the matching `_cover.jpg`. If it doesn't, pick the frame with the large headline (the first 1–2 seconds).
- **TikTok Studio (computer, tiktok.com/tiktokstudio):** upload the video, then **Edit cover** → upload the image, if that option is shown for your account.
- Add a short cover title only if needed: the headline is already in the image.

## Publishing order & tips
1. **Post in order EP1 → EP6**, one episode every 1–2 days, so each video gets its own reach.
2. **Timing:** start with the evening after school, then check **TikTok Studio → Analytics → Followers → Follower activity** after a few posts to find the best hour for your account.
3. **Pin** EP1 (the series start) and EP6 (the revision) on your profile.
4. **Playlist:** if your account has *Playlists*, create "Bab 1 – Warisan Negara Bangsa" and add all six.
5. **First comment (pin it):** `📌 Siri penuh Bab 1: EP1 Intro ➜ EP2 (1.1) ➜ EP3 (1.2) ➜ EP4 (1.3) ➜ EP5 (1.4) ➜ EP6 Ulang kaji + kuiz`
6. **Reply with video:** use students' answers or questions in the comments (especially on EP6) as ideas for short follow-up videos.
7. **Upload the `.srt` file** (`output/subtitles/`) as captions if TikTok asks; the on-screen captions are already burned in.

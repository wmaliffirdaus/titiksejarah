# TitikSejarah — Official Style Guide (from Episode 4 onward)

> **TitikSejarah — Malaysian History**
>
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Style** | Historical documentary |
> | **Mood** | Serious + Emotional + Nostalgic |
> | **Visual** | Archival photographs + Historical maps + Timeline animation |
> | **Pacing** | Moderate + Suspense-driven |

This replaces the earlier "doodle notebook" look (EP1–EP3). The brand palette, the official profile picture and the short-form retention rules stay.

## 1. Style – historical documentary
- Tell history as a **true story with stakes**: people, decisions, consequences. It should feel like a documentary, not a classroom lesson or a cartoon.
- Narration is a **storyteller-historian**, not a teacher listing points.
- Every fact stays **textbook-accurate (KSSM Sejarah)**. Drama comes from framing and pacing, never from invented details.
- Clearly label reconstructions or illustrative images (e.g. "Ilustrasi", "Lukisan semula").

## 2. Mood – serious, emotional, nostalgic
- **Tone:** grounded and respectful. No jokes, cartoon sound effects, emoji-style graphics or "hype" energy.
- **Emotion:** focus on the human side, such as ambition, loyalty, betrayal, loss and pride in heritage.
- **Nostalgia:** warm, aged visual treatment (sepia/film grain, paper textures, slow push-ins); a sense of "our past".

## 3. Visual – archival photos, historical maps, timeline animation
- **Archival photographs:** historical photos, artefacts, manuscripts, inscriptions, paintings and museum pieces from sources whose licence is checked (public domain or Creative Commons, e.g. Wikimedia Commons, museum open-access collections). Show credits in the source line.
- **Historical maps:** period maps or clean modern maps with animated territories, routes and pins.
- **Timeline animation:** a recurring timeline bar that marks the year or century of each event and moves as the story moves.
- **Motion:** slow Ken Burns pans and zooms on photos, gentle parallax, film-burn or dissolve transitions; never static for long.
- **Text:** minimal, elegant titles (serif or documentary typeface); keywords highlighted subtly. Subtitles stay readable and synced.
- **Brand continuity:** navy `#172C63`, gold `#F5C928`, heritage red `#C93436`, cream `#F3EBDD` used as accents. The **official profile picture (unaltered)** is shown on the closing card.
- **Doodles:** retired as the main style. Simple line icons may still appear inside infographic moments.

## 4. Pacing – moderate, suspense-driven
- **Moderate speed:** clear, deliberate narration, slightly slower and weightier than the EP2/EP3 hype pace, but never dragging.
- **Suspense structure:** open with a question or tension, delay the answer, build to a reveal, and end on a cliffhanger or reflective question.
- **Purposeful pauses** before reveals: short, intentional silences with the music swelling.
- Visual changes follow the story beats rather than constant quick cuts.
- **Duration:** still 35–60 s per episode (ideal 45–55 s for documentary pacing); split topics instead of rushing.

## 5. Audio
- **Voice:** Asyraf (ElevenLabs, Multilingual v2, Malaysian accent), in a **serious, warm documentary narration**. Use lower style energy than EP3, slightly higher stability, near-natural speed (≈1.0–1.05×) and controlled emotional emphasis on names, dates and turning points.
- **Music:** cinematic and nostalgic, such as soft piano, strings, ambient pads, low drums for tension and a gentle swell at reveals. Always about 20–25 dB under the voice.
- **Sound design:** subtle and realistic (wind, waves, distant crowd, paper rustle, low impact hits), no cartoon pops.

## 6. Retention rules that still apply (learned from EP1–EP3)
- **Frame 1 must be a complete, striking image** with the hook text. Never a blank or slowly building frame.
- The voice starts within the first ~0.2 s; the hook lands within 3 s; no greetings or channel intro before the hook.
- Structure: **0–3 s hook → 3–10 s context → story → takeaway or reveal → closing question or teaser**.
- Every sentence must earn its place; no lecture lists.
- End with the brand card (official DP, "TITIK SEJARAH", "Kenali sejarah, fahami masa kini.", "Follow untuk lebih banyak ilmu sejarah.") while the closing question is spoken.

## 7. Production prerequisites
- **Image access:** Wikimedia Commons hosts are allowed on the `titiksejarah` environment but were rate-limited from the cloud session. Options: retry with slower requests, use other open-access collections, or **you supply photos** (textbook photos, your own museum photos, or licensed stock). Sources and licences are recorded per episode.
- **Textbook:** follow KSSM Sejarah order. For a new chapter, confirm the subtopic list from the textbook (send photos or screenshots of the contents page) before scripting.

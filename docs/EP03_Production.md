## 1. Episode title

## 2. Three alternative hooks

| # | Hook | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | "Perkampungan nelayan kecil… jadi **empayar** dalam masa kurang 100 tahun!" | Surprising transformation, opens a "how?" gap, leads straight into "Apa rahsianya?" | – |
| B | "Siam serang Melaka. Siapa menang?" | Instant conflict | Spoils the climax; the first 30 s (wilayah, definitions) would feel like a detour |
| C | "Lawan Sultan Melaka? Itu bukan sekadar salah… itu derhaka!" | Emotional, memorable | Covers only kedaulatan, so the wilayah half loses the hook's promise |

## 3. Selected hook: **A**
It is a surprising fact that is true to the textbook (Melaka began as a fishing settlement around 1400 and was a major power by the reign of Sultan Mansur Syah, 1459–1477, so well under 100 years). It also creates the question the whole episode answers: *what made Melaka so strong?* Frame 1 already shows the whole transformation (hut and boat → arrow → palace with crown), with the headline "KAMPUNG NELAYAN ➜ EMPAYAR".

## 6. AI voiceover delivery (as configured)
- **Voice:** Asyraf, ElevenLabs Multilingual v2 (keeps the Malaysian final schwa: *Melakə*).
- **Settings:** style **0.60** (more emotion), stability **0.28**, model speed **1.08**, plus a **1.11** time-stretch (pitch kept).
- **Measured pace:** **15.3 chars/s = 1.11× EP2** (target 1.10–1.15×).
- **Delivery by line:**
  - Hook: rising excitement, a little stress on "empayar".
  - Questions ("Apa rahsianya?", "Caranya?", "Lawan Sultan?"): short and punchy, then answered immediately.
  - Names and places: crisp emphasis (the bold words in the script).
  - "…" marks only two deliberate short pauses: before "jadi empayar" and before "serangan itu berjaya dipatahkan".
- **Pronunciation:** numbers are spoken in Malay ("seratus", "kelima belas").

# TitikSejarah — Identiti Jenama

> *"Kenali sejarah, fahami masa kini."*

Canonical brand summary. **The visual and audio style for videos is defined in `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md`** (historical documentary; serious, emotional and nostalgic; archival photos, maps and timeline animation; moderate, suspense-driven pacing). This file covers audience, voice, formats and rules that apply to every product. It does not repeat the style guide.

## Misi

Menjadikan Sejarah Malaysia mudah difahami dan menarik untuk murid Tingkatan 4 dan 5 melalui video dokumentari pendek, dokumentari panjang, nota dan bank soalan yang **tepat dari segi fakta dan selari dengan KSSM**. Murid patut *faham dan peduli* (sebab, akibat, kepentingan), bukan sekadar menghafal tarikh.

## Khalayak

| Segmen | Keperluan | Implikasi kandungan |
|---|---|---|
| Murid Tingkatan 4–5 (15–17 tahun) — utama | Faham sebab-akibat, ingat fakta kunci, lulus SPM | Cerita jelas, contoh konkrit, kaitan dengan soalan SPM |
| Calon SPM menjelang peperiksaan | Ulang kaji pantas | Ringkasan padat, kata kunci, kuiz |
| Guru Sejarah | Bahan bantu mengajar yang boleh dipercayai | Sumber dinyatakan, padanan bab/subtopik |
| Ibu bapa / umum | Sejarah yang sahih dan menarik | Penceritaan, foto arkib |

Most viewers find TikTok videos on the For You page, on a phone, often muted. **The first second decides retention.** EP1 data: average watch time 4.6 s of 71 s, drop-off at 0:01 (see `docs/Retention_Analysis_EP1_and_Plan.md`).

## Personaliti

- **Storyteller-historian**, not a teacher listing points. Grounded, warm, respectful.
- **Honest about uncertainty.** Drama comes from framing and pacing, never from invented details.
- **Patriotic without propaganda.** Lessons come from facts, not slogans.
- No jokes, cartoon SFX or hype energy on serious topics (per STYLE_GUIDE §2).

## Perkara yang TitikSejarah tidak lakukan

- Invent dates, people, quotations, treaties or photos.
- Claim generated questions are real SPM papers, predictions or leaks.
- Use clickbait the video doesn't deliver.
- Copy textbook pages or commercial reference books wholesale.
- Publish images without a verified licence and on-screen credit.
